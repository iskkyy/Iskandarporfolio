About Me

Hi My Name
Iskandar

I am a **Web Designer** with a primary focus on crafting **clean, efficient, and visually quiet digital experiences**. My work is rooted in the belief that effective design is often the simplest design. I leverage minimalist principles to ensure the user interface is intuitive, the information is accessible, and the overall aesthetic is modern and sophisticated. Having successfully accomplished many projects, my expertise spans various platforms. While I am deeply passionate about **minimalist theme design**, I am always ready to **step outside my comfort zone** to master new styles and visual identities required to precisely fulfill the unique needs of my client.

Projects

AR Card

This project explores the development of an Augmented Reality (AR) business card, transitioning a simplistic name card into an interactive digital experience. Designed to trigger a series of experiences like floating UI elements, interactive 3D models and video playback, and outcome is a dynamic, self-serving card that enhances the traditional exchange of contact information, serving as a memorable and unique brand asset for the digital age.

Avatar Character

This project required the creation of unique avatar design, where I seek to explore the elements of familiarity and pop culture. The semi-cartoonist aspect provides a detailed model and expressive rig, allowing for a realistic range of motion and personality. The visual design is vibrant and inviting, reflecting key themes of the project.

VR Game

In this project we are task to create a VR escape room. The 'Silent Scourge' offers a chilling and unique gaming experience. Its core appeal lies in its immersive storytelling, coupled with intuitive controls and spatial sound design. The game challenges players to solve complex puzzles and uncover the truth in a dimly lit, eerie environment. Players will descent into the quiet horror of the unknown.
